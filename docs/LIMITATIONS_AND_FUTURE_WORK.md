# Limitations and future work

[Borrowed Understanding](../README.md)

## Interpretation

- **Sampling.** A small convenience sample offers limited coverage of the people and contexts in which AI-assisted learning occurs. Participant flow, missingness and prior exposure to related instruments matter when interpreting any pattern.
- **Comparison.** Everyone reads the guide. Without a randomized comparator, observed changes can also reflect practice, task familiarity, order, fatigue or attention. Alternating the case order helps distribute case effects while leaving those other explanations open.
- **Transfer.** The library and concert items were designed to be comparable, with equivalence still to be checked. The museum challenge changes the task and has no pre-guide counterpart. Its responses document performance on an unfamiliar problem and cannot by themselves quantify a transfer gain.
- **Measurement.** The confidence scales are ordinal self-reports, and the reasoning dimensions are case-specific judgments. A difference between scale values would have no established interpretation as a confidence–ability gap. Preliminary automated assessments require human review before supporting substantive claims.
- **Version changes.** Prior participation in LL-1.0 or a related instrument could affect familiarity and strategy. Revisions also changed what BU-E1.0 measured. Earlier feasibility observations and current pilot records need version-specific interpretation.

## Work before reporting

A later report should account for participant flow and missing observations by instrument revision and case order, describe prior exposure where review permits, and present confidence and reasoning separately before exploring their correspondence. Human scoring should document masking and agreement where available. Any analysis choices made after examining the data should be identified as exploratory.

A later comparison study could estimate the guide’s contribution using a suitable randomized comparator, with case-pair difficulty and scoring reliability checked before wider use.

The planned report is *Perceived Understanding and Independent Causal Reasoning Following AI-Generated Explanations: An Exploratory Human Pilot Study*. Release of an empirical report or aggregate results is conditional on scientific and privacy review.
