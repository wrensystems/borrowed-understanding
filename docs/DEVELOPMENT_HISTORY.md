# Development history

[Borrowed Understanding](../README.md)

## BU-0.8 · Original submission

The Borrowed Understanding proposal advanced to the next stage of the Generation AI Research Prize at the University of Oxford’s Reuben College. The initial application stated that no human observations had been collected. This was a proposal about whether confidence following AI-style guidance aligns with independent reasoning when evidence changes.

The preserved BU-0.8 candidate translated that question into fictional learning scenarios with standardized guidance. It asked for a judgment and reason, a reconsideration after a fixed guide, an independent revision after new information, and a transfer judgment. Optional self-reports asked about perceived understanding of the fictional rules. Local rehearsals used synthetic material and a fixed mock guide. [Generation AI Research Prize](https://www.generationai.world/generation-ai-research-prize)

## LL-1.0 · The Last Light

LL-1.0 served as a human feasibility instrument. In a single fictional setting, participants answered the same causal question before and after a prewritten AI guide, then responded to corrected evidence and a separate model-construction problem. The repeated item made changes in an answer visible while leaving open how much repetition itself shaped the second response.

The feasibility stage preceded the move to two distinct matched cases and separate confidence measurements. Human observations from LL-1.0 pertain to that instrument. Its outcomes are outside this public release.

## BU-E1.0 · The Other Explanation

BU-E1.0 uses the library and concert cases as a counterbalanced pair. Each participant encounters both, with the fixed guide between them. The museum challenge introduces a separate situation and provides a distinct written observation of reasoning under unfamiliar evidence.

Later BU-E1.0 revisions added answer-specific confidence alongside general confidence and refined the fixed causal guide. The implementation preserves the instrument revision under which an answer was collected. Any earlier session retains its original guide and measurement schedule. The original revision did not collect AC1 or AC2. Post-submission automated assessment and the optional Deep Echo reflection follow separate processes.

The versions document successive design decisions. Changes in stimuli, guide, response requirements and confidence questions limit comparisons across versions. Any later report should state who was eligible for analysis under each revision and how prior exposure was handled.
